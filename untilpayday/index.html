Budget by Your Paycheck

Make your money last until payday.

Payday Left is a simple paycheck-based budget planner built around one question:

How much can I safely spend today and still make it to my next payday?

Instead of forcing your budget into the 1st through the end of the month, Payday Left follows the way you actually get paid.

Set your paycheck, upcoming bills, savings, and next payday. Payday Left does the rest.

SEE WHAT'S SAFE TO SPEND

Your Today screen shows three things that matter:

  • Money left until payday
  • Days until your next paycheck
  • What you can safely spend today

Spend less today and the extra is naturally spread across the days ahead. Spend more and your future daily amount adjusts.

BUILT AROUND YOUR PAY SCHEDULE

Payday Left supports:

  • Weekly pay
  • Every two weeks
  • Twice a month
  • Monthly pay
  • Irregular paydays

Weekend payday adjustments are supported too.

BILLS ARE ACCOUNTED FOR BEFORE YOU SPEND

Add upcoming bills and Payday Left reserves that money before calculating your daily spending amount.

When a planned bill is paid, only the difference between the planned and actual amount changes your available spending money.

NO BANK LOGIN

Payday Left does not connect to your bank.

There is no account, bank connection, subscription or financial-account syncing.

WIDGETS AT A GLANCE

Use Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets to keep your current pay cycle visible without opening the app.

YOUR BUDGET RECORDS STAY ON YOUR DEVICE

Income, expenses, bills, savings amounts and notes are stored locally on your iPhone.

Advertising services may process information separately according to your privacy choices.

SIMPLE ON PURPOSE

Payday Left is not an investing app, banking app, credit tracker or complicated financial dashboard.

It is a small tool for answering one everyday question: Can I afford to spend this today?

Payday Left provides planning estimates based on the information you enter. It does not access, hold, transfer or manage your money.
