Support and frequently asked questions

What is Payday Left?

Payday Left is a paycheck-based spending planner. It estimates how much money remains before your next payday and how much you can safely spend today based on the information you enter.

Does Payday Left connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection or financial-account login. You enter the amounts you want to track yourself.

Where are my budget records stored?

Your paycheck amounts, expenses, bills, savings amounts and notes are stored locally on your iPhone. A small summary is also stored in the app's iOS App Group container so the widget can display your budget.

Advertising services may process separate technical/advertising information according to your privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which pay schedules are supported?

  • Weekly
  • Every 2 weeks
  • Twice a month
  • Monthly
  • Irregular/manual payday

Weekend payday adjustment can also be configured.

What does Safe Today mean?

Safe Today is an estimate of what remains available for flexible spending today while spreading the rest of your available money across the days until your next payday.

Bills you have already planned are reserved before Safe Today is calculated.

Why did paying a planned bill barely change what is left?

That is intentional. The planned amount was already reserved before payment. When you mark the bill paid, the reserve is replaced with the actual payment. Only any difference between the planned and actual amount changes your remaining spending money.

What happens if I spend more than Safe Today?

The app does not block spending. Safe Today falls to zero for the current day and the remaining money is redistributed across future days.

Why is my currency locked?

Payday Left does not perform currency conversion. Locking the currency prevents old bills, carryover and new cycles from silently mixing incompatible currencies. Reset local data to start a new budget in another currency.

Can I use the app without notifications?

Yes. Payday reminders are optional and the budgeting features work normally when notification permission is denied.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is part of v1. The free app uses limited interstitial advertising at natural transition points. Ads are never required to calculate or view your budget.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and choose Reset All Data. You can also delete the app from your device, subject to normal iOS backup/system behavior.
