Privacy Policy for Payday Left

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Payday Left is designed to help you plan your spending between paydays while keeping your personal budget records on your device.

Budget Data

The financial information you enter into Payday Left, including paycheck amounts, expenses, bills, savings amounts, transaction notes and pay-cycle information, is stored locally on your device.

Payday Left does not require an account and does not connect to your bank. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your personal budget records.

Advertising

Payday Left uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising.

Google and its advertising technologies may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, advertising information, app interactions, crash information and performance information.

In this version, Payday Left disables Publisher first-party ID and publisher ad personalization before initializing Google Mobile Ads, and limits requested ad content to a General rating.

Payday Left does not intentionally send the paycheck amounts, expense amounts, bill names, savings amounts or transaction notes you enter to Google Mobile Ads for advertising purposes.

Privacy Choices and Consent

Where required, Payday Left uses Google's User Messaging Platform to provide privacy and consent choices. Advertising requests are made only when the advertising SDK indicates that ads may be requested under the applicable consent state.

If privacy options are required for your region, they can be accessed from Settings in the app.

App Tracking Transparency

Payday Left does not intentionally request access to Apple's Identifier for Advertisers through an App Tracking Transparency permission prompt in this version.

Notifications

If you enable payday reminders, notification scheduling is handled locally on your device. Notification permission is optional.

Widgets

Payday Left stores a small budget summary in the app's shared iOS App Group container so Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can display current budget information. This information remains on your device.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your budget records remain on your device until you remove them. You can erase the app's locally stored budget data using Reset All Data in Settings.

Deleting the app also removes locally stored app data subject to normal iOS system behavior and Apple-managed backups.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal budget records. We do not send your entered paycheck amounts, bills, expenses, savings amounts or notes to our own server.

Third-party advertising services may process their own data to provide, measure, secure or personalize advertising according to applicable law and your privacy choices.

Children

Payday Left is a general financial-planning utility and is not specifically designed for children. Advertising configuration limits ad content to a general-audience rating.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features, third-party services or legal requirements change.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the Support link provided on the Payday Left App Store product page.
