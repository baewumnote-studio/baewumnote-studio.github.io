What does PowerLeft do?

PowerLeft estimates how long a battery or portable power station can run selected devices, and estimates how much capacity is needed for a target runtime.

Does PowerLeft connect to my power station?

No. PowerLeft is a brand-neutral offline calculator. It does not use Bluetooth or manufacturer APIs.

Why can actual runtime differ?

Actual results vary with device cycling, inverter overhead, conversion losses, temperature, battery condition, startup surge, and manufacturer limits.

Where is my data stored?

Saved batteries, custom devices, and setups are stored locally on your device. No PowerLeft account is required.

How do I delete saved data?

Open Settings → Saved Data → Delete Saved Data.

Ads and privacy

PowerLeft uses Google AdMob interstitial ads. Where required, Google UMP provides regional privacy choices. Core calculations work even if ads are unavailable.

Critical equipment

Do not use PowerLeft as the only backup-planning method for medically necessary equipment. Follow manufacturer guidance and test your actual setup in advance.
