Calculator data

Battery profiles, custom devices, and saved setups are stored locally on your device. PowerLeft does not require an account and does not send these calculator records to a PowerLeft server.

Advertising

PowerLeft uses Google AdMob interstitial advertising. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google Mobile Ads configuration, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP-derived general location, advertising interactions, usage, and diagnostic information.

Privacy choices

Where required, PowerLeft uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before requesting ads. You can also revisit available privacy choices from Settings inside the app.

Tracking permission

The current MVP does not intentionally request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If a future version introduces tracking as defined by Apple, the app and this policy will be updated before release.

Retention

Calculator data remains on your device until you delete it in Settings or remove/reset the app. Advertising-data retention follows Google and its partners' policies.

Children

PowerLeft is not designed or marketed as a children's app.

Critical equipment

PowerLeft provides electrical planning estimates only. Do not use it as your only backup-planning method for medically necessary equipment.
